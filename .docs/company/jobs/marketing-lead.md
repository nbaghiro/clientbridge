# Marketing Lead

## About Clientbridge

Clientbridge is one app for service businesses in Canada: pet groomers, salons, cleaners, personal trainers, tutors and home repair teams. It handles online booking with deposits, the calendar, client records, invoices and estimates, card payments online and with Tap to Pay, an online shop, packages, memberships and gift cards, staff pay, two-way messaging and Canadian sales tax. It runs on the web, iPhone and Android, and the schedule and client list keep working when the connection drops.

We're a small founding team getting ready to launch, and most of the product is already built.

## The role

You'll own how service businesses first hear about Clientbridge and why they decide to try it. You'll set up our marketing from the start: the message, the channels, the content and the numbers that tell us what's working.

## What you'll be marketing

The product has a clear story for each trade, and plenty of real detail to show:

- A groomer's booking page takes a deposit, and the deposit is applied to the invoice automatically.
- A salon sells products at the desk and online from the same catalog, with stock counts that drop as they sell.
- A cleaning business sends estimates that turn into invoices when the client accepts them.
- A trainer runs classes with capacity limits, plus packages and monthly memberships.
- Every business takes cards with Tap to Pay on their own phone, and sees the sales tax they owe for their province.

Our public pages carry each business's own logo and colours, so every booking and payment page is also a small advertisement for the business, and for the product behind it.

## What you'll do

- Shape our positioning and messaging, by trade and for Canada as a whole.
- Run the website, search, content and social channels, and grow the waitlist into trials.
- Create trade-specific landing pages, guides, short videos and customer stories.
- Set up the funnel from first visit to trial to paid account, and measure each step.
- Plan launches and campaigns with the founders and the sales lead.
- Test referral offers, partnerships with trade associations and local communities.

## What you'll bring

- Experience marketing a product to small businesses or consumers, ideally software.
- Strong, plain writing, and an eye for design and video.
- Comfort with analytics, search and paid channels, and the judgement to know which numbers matter.
- The ability to plan a campaign and also make the assets yourself.
- Interest in the everyday work of small service businesses.

## Nice to have

- Experience at an early-stage startup.
- Experience marketing in Canada, including in French.
- Links to communities in grooming, beauty, fitness, cleaning or home services.

## How we work

We're a small team, so you'll work directly with the founders and own your area from day one. We write the way we'd explain things to a customer in person: clearly, warmly and without hype. You'll have a meaningful share in the company.
