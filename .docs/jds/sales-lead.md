# Sales Lead

## About Clientbridge

Clientbridge is one app for service businesses in Canada: pet groomers, salons, cleaners, personal trainers, tutors and home repair teams. It handles online booking with deposits, the calendar, client records, invoices and estimates, card payments online and with Tap to Pay, an online shop, packages, memberships and gift cards, staff pay, two-way messaging and Canadian sales tax. It runs on the web, iPhone and Android, and the schedule and client list keep working when the connection drops.

We're a small founding team getting ready to launch, and most of the product is already built.

## The role

You'll bring in our first customers. That means finding the right businesses, showing them how Clientbridge fits the way they work, and staying with them until they're running their day in it. What you learn will shape what we build and how we sell from here.

## What you'll be selling

Most of the businesses we serve run on three or four separate tools today: one for booking, one for invoices, a card reader, a spreadsheet for tax. Clientbridge puts these in one place:

- A booking page with the business's own logo and colours, which takes a deposit and can sit on their website.
- A calendar that knows each service's length, price and buffer, with recurring visits and reminders by email and text.
- Invoices that pick up the services from the visit, add the right tax and go out with a link the client can pay from.
- Tap to Pay on the phone they already have, so there's no card reader to buy.
- GST, HST, PST and QST worked out on every sale, with the amount owed always on the Today screen.
- Packages, memberships, gift cards and an online shop, all in the same checkout.

There are two plans: Premium at $49.99 a month for an owner working alone or with a few staff, and Team at $99.99 a month with the whole staff included. Both come with every feature and a 14-day free trial.

## What you'll do

- Build and work a pipeline of local service businesses, starting with the trades we know best.
- Run demos using a ready-made demo business, and tailor them to each trade.
- Close deals and stay with each new customer until their account is set up and in daily use.
- Keep a clear record of every lead and deal, and report what's working each week.
- Bring customer feedback to the product team in a clear, prioritised form.
- Help write the sales playbook, pricing tests and referral offers as we grow.
