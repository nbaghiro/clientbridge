# Customer Success Lead

## About Clientbridge

Clientbridge is one app for service businesses in Canada: pet groomers, salons, cleaners, personal trainers, tutors and home repair teams. It handles online booking with deposits, the calendar, client records, invoices and estimates, card payments online and with Tap to Pay, an online shop, packages, memberships and gift cards, staff pay, two-way messaging and Canadian sales tax. It runs on the web, iPhone and Android, and the schedule and client list keep working when the connection drops.

We're a small founding team getting ready to launch, and most of the product is already built.

## The role

You'll make sure every new customer gets set up well and keeps using Clientbridge. You'll guide businesses through onboarding, answer their questions, and turn what you hear into clear feedback for the product team.

## What you'll help customers set up

A business is ready to run its day in Clientbridge once a few things are in place, and you'll know each one well:

- The business profile, branding and booking page, embedded on their website if they have one.
- Services with their length, price and buffer, plus classes, packages, memberships and gift cards.
- Staff invites, roles and working hours.
- Their payments account, so card payments and Tap to Pay pay out to their bank.
- Sales tax for their province, with a tax class on each item.
- Their client list, imported and tidied, with intake forms and waivers ready to sign online.

Before anything goes out to a client, such as an invoice, a reminder or a reschedule notice, the app shows exactly what will be sent and asks the owner to confirm. Teaching customers to trust that habit is part of the job.

## What you'll do

- Run onboarding calls and help each business finish its setup.
- Answer support questions by email, chat and phone, quickly and kindly.
- Watch how new accounts are doing and reach out before small problems become reasons to leave.
- Write help articles, checklists and short videos for common tasks.
- Report bugs and feature requests clearly, with the steps and context the engineers need.
- Help shape pricing, onboarding and retention as we learn what customers need.

## What you'll bring

- Experience in customer success, support or account management, ideally for software.
- Patience and clear explanations for people who aren't technical.
- Good organisation, so no customer or question falls through the cracks.
- Comfort learning a broad product in depth.
- Calm and good judgement when a customer is having a bad day.

## Nice to have

- Experience with booking, point of sale or invoicing software.
- Knowledge of Canadian sales tax or small business bookkeeping.
- French.

## How we work

We're a small team, so you'll work directly with the founders and see your feedback turn into product changes quickly. You'll have a meaningful share in the company.
