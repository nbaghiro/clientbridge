# Founding Product Designer

## About Clientbridge

Clientbridge is one app for service businesses in Canada: pet groomers, salons, cleaners, personal trainers, tutors and home repair teams. It handles online booking with deposits, the calendar, client records, invoices and estimates, card payments online and with Tap to Pay, an online shop, packages, memberships and gift cards, staff pay, two-way messaging and Canadian sales tax. It runs on the web, iPhone and Android, and the schedule and client list keep working when the connection drops.

We're a small founding team getting ready to launch, and most of the product is already built.

## The role

You'll be the first designer on the team. You'll own how Clientbridge looks and feels across the web app, the iPhone and Android apps, the public booking and payment pages and the marketing site, and you'll keep them consistent as the product grows.

## What you'll design

The product is broad, and each part has its own design problem:

- **Today**, the screen an owner opens every morning: the day's visits, what's owed, and the sales tax set aside.
- **The calendar**, with day, week, month and per-staff views, drag to reschedule, recurring visits and classes.
- **Invoices and estimates**, shown as real paper beside the record, with a preview of every email and text before it's sent.
- **Checkout**, from Tap to Pay at the counter to the client's own payment page.
- **Public pages** in each business's logo and colours, which clients use to book, pay, sign forms and manage their visits.
- **Settings and onboarding** that get a new business from sign-up to its first booking quickly.

There's already a design system: shared tokens for colour and type, a set of components built twice (once for the web, once for native mobile) behind the same props, and a design lab for trying ideas against real screens. Every string comes from one message catalog, so copy is part of the design work too.

## What you'll do

- Design new features end to end, from flows and wireframes to polished screens on web and mobile.
- Grow and maintain the design system with the engineers.
- Talk to customers, watch them use the product, and fix what confuses them.
- Write clear, plain interface copy.
- Design the marketing site and trade pages with the marketing lead.

## What you'll bring

- A portfolio of shipped product design for web and mobile apps.
- Strong interaction design, visual design and typography.
- Experience building or working within a design system.
- Comfort working closely with engineers, and enough front-end knowledge to talk about trade-offs.
- Care for small details, such as empty states, errors, loading and phone widths.

## Nice to have

- Experience designing booking, point of sale, invoicing or other business tools.
- Front-end skills with React or Tailwind.
- Motion or illustration skills.

## How we work

We're a small team, so you'll work directly with the founders and engineers, and your designs will ship quickly. You'll have a meaningful share in the company.
