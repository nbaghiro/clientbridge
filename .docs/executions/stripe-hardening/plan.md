# Stripe hardening plan

Status: in progress. Branch `fix/stripe-hardening` in `/Users/naib/Documents/code/clientbridge-stripe`, cut from `origin/main` at `a9366132` on 2026-10-26.

This execution fixes the findings from the October 26 review of every Stripe flow: Connect onboarding and the KYC mirror, charges, Customers, memberships, refunds, disputes, webhooks, reconciliation, payouts, platform plans and the Stripe configuration in infra. PR #42 merged on October 19 with its own docs still listing open blockers, and the scheduled recovery job was removed after it. This plan picks up those blockers too.

[Schema proposals](schema.md) holds the one schema change this needs, for approval. [Task queue](tasks.json) holds the ordered tasks and their acceptance. [Progress](progress.md) records what has been done and the evidence.

## Principles for this work

- **Test first.** Each finding gets a failing regression before its fix, through the real app and Postgres (`make test-backend`, or `uv run python -m e2e.database pytest ...` for a targeted run). Fakes stay at the Stripe boundary only.
- **The 4-part matrix** for every new or changed endpoint: happy path, each 4xx, tenant and role isolation, and idempotency or replay.
- **Layering.** Routers stay thin, services own their queries through `scoped()`, role gates live in the service method, and job bodies live in their concept's service with only the schedule in `tasks/worker.py`. Ledger primitives stay in `services/ledger.py`; Stripe orchestration stays in `services/payments.py`.
- **One file per concept.** New tests go in the existing concept files (`test_payments_refunds.py`, `test_ledger_drift.py`, `test_payments_disputes.py` if disputes outgrow refunds). No new layer files unless a new concept appears.
- **Comments are rare and one line.** Names carry the meaning. No narration and no section banners.
- **One current implementation.** No compatibility paths. Demo and staging data are reseeded rather than migrated where a journal shape changes.
- **Copy** goes through `en-CA.json` and `en_CA.po`, and every new API error has a stable `error` code that clients render through `failureMessage`.
- **Schema** changes only after explicit approval of the exact change in [schema.md](schema.md).
- **Real flows.** The Stripe browser lane drives each money flow through the real interface against the Stripe sandbox and checks the journals with the read-only observers (see [End-to-end coverage](#end-to-end-coverage)).

## Decisions

All decided on 2026-10-26. Q4 and Q6 to Q11 took the recommended defaults.

| ID  | Question                                                       | Answer                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q1  | Is the platform fee charged on tips?                           | **Decided: yes.** The platform fee applies to every transaction, tips included. Today's behaviour stays.                                                |
| Q2  | Does a membership charge a platform fee?                       | **Decided: yes,** through `application_fee_percent` at the same effective rate as a card charge.                                                         |
| Q3  | Is the platform fee returned when we void an order for stock?  | **Decided: yes.**                                                                                                                                       |
| Q4  | Where does money lost twice (refund, then a lost dispute) go?  | **Decided:** a `loss` account category, so sales and tax never drop below what was collected ([S1](schema.md)).                                         |
| Q5  | Who may open account management and payouts?                   | **Decided: owners and admins.** Today's behaviour stays.                                                                                                |
| Q6  | What happens to staff commission on a refund?                  | **Decided:** a partial refund reduces a pending commission in proportion; approved or paid commission is not taken back. Documented.                 |
| Q7  | What happens to client memberships when the business lapses?   | **Decided:** they keep billing, the owner can cancel them while lapsed, and the owner is emailed that they are still charging.                         |
| Q8  | Can any staff member charge a saved card at the desk?          | **Decided:** yes for sales and deposits at the desk; invoice saved-card charges stay with owners and admins. Documented.                                 |
| Q9  | Can a lapsed business collect a deposit from a manage link?    | **Decided:** yes, the same as paying an invoice, since the booking already exists. New bookings stay blocked.                                           |
| Q10 | Does a price change move existing members?                     | **Decided:** no; existing members keep their price and the item page says so.                                                                         |
| Q11 | Who carries the Stripe fee on an orphan refund?                | **Decided:** the business, as today, since Stripe takes it from the connected account and moving it to us needs a transfer. Revisit if it happens.     |
| Q12 | Which currencies do prices allow?                              | **Decided: CAD only, built to extend.** One `SUPPORTED_CURRENCIES` set in `services/ledger.py` gates items, charge builders and journals; ledger accounts are already keyed by currency, so adding one is a constant change plus fee and report checks. |
