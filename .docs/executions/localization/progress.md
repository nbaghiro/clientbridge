## F30 to F33: backend catalog (completed)

- `core/locale.py` loads `locales/en_CA.po` with Babel's `read_po`, compiles it in memory and caches one `GNUTranslations` per locale. `locale_for(business=, client=, user=)` returns `en_CA`, or `en_XA` when the `PSEUDO_LOCALE` setting is on. It also provides `message`, `plural`, `format_money` (the amount's currency), `format_number`, `format_percent`, `format_day_and_time` (business time zone) and `currency_for(business)`.
- `locales/en_CA.po` is the hand-edited source with 80 keyed messages; `messages.pot` and `en_XA.po` are generated by `scripts/gen_messages.py` (`make gen-messages`, part of `codegen-check`), which also fails on missing, unused or empty English messages. The wheel ships the `.po` and `.pot` files. Babel 2.17.0 was added.
- Every builder in `services/notifications.py` reads the catalog. The password reset, email verification, staff invite and order reminder copy moved into that file from `auth.py`, `staff.py` and `returning.py`. The SMS opt-out keyword is passed as a value so it cannot be translated.
- A snapshot of 45 builder outputs is identical before and after apart from the F33 fixes: amounts read `$48.00` instead of `$48.00 CAD`, thousands are grouped, negative amounts are correct (`-$12.50`; previously `$-13.50`), other currencies read `US$48.00`, and dates read `Friday, October 9 at 2:30 p.m.` in the business time zone instead of `2026-10-09 at 14:30`.
- Gift card emails use the purchase payment's currency; estimate emails use `currency_for(business)`. `services/public.py` and `services/billing.py` still hardcode `CAD` for estimates and are left for F34 to F36.
- `tests/test_notifications_locale.py` adds 20 tests, including a guard that every builder is rendered and an `en_XA` check that no unconverted text remains. Full backend suite: 1,382 passed, 95.32% branch coverage; ruff, mypy strict and the structure check pass.

## F20 to F25, F34 to F36: formatter, time zones, tax names, errors (completed)

- `format.ts` and `datetime.ts` are the one formatter. Every function uses `activeLocale()`, an explicit time zone for dates and an explicit currency for money (zero-decimal currencies included). `formatMoney(cents, currency)` has about 365 call sites; records use their own currency and the rest use `BUSINESS_CURRENCY = "CAD"`, since businesses have no currency column. Ranges and day-and-time compositions are catalog messages rather than `formatRange`, because the mobile polyfill and V8 disagree on its output. Relative times and durations stay catalog messages.
- All 31 locale literals, 41 `toLocale*` calls, 3 `new Intl` and the display `toFixed` sites go through the formatter; the remaining ones are inside the formatter, the debug panels, signature coordinates and an e2e file. The 38 `prefix="$"` fields use `currencyAffix`, with a new `suffix` slot on `TextField`. `parseCents` and `parseNumber` read separators and currency signs from Intl.
- Staff surfaces read the business zone through `BusinessZoneContext`; device-zone getters are gone from display and grouping, including SQL `date('now')`. Every public payload carries `timezone` (additive contract change, `make gen-api` run), and Connect shows a zone note on booking, manage, the landing page and shop pickup when the visitor's zone differs. Hour cycle comes from Intl and week start from a per-locale table (Monday for en-CA, since CLDR says Sunday). The business screen on web and mobile has a time zone select; `invalid_timezone` maps to a catalog message.
- Tax names render from `taxes.names.*` through `taxName`, `taxList` and `taxRateLabel`; the English `name` is gone from `PROVINCE_TAX_RATES`, `/v1/tax-rates` and the report summary. No table stores an English tax name. Invoices are still created with `currency="CAD"` in billing as data.
- The api-client throws errors carrying `status` and the body's `error` code; app-core maps them through `failedStatus`, `failedCode`, `failureMessage` and `refusal(res)`, replacing nine public error classes. No client renders an API message. The backend added codes `shop_not_ready`, `pickup_window_taken`, `out_of_stock`, `mixed_currencies` and `invalid_request` for validation failures.
- Mobile polyfills now also force `@formatjs/intl-numberformat` 9.4.2 and `@formatjs/intl-datetimeformat` 7.7.0 with English data and all time zones (about 1.3 MB raw), since Apple's Hermes lacks `formatToParts`, unit styles and `formatRange` and prints "PM". Zone offsets and formatted results are cached for Hermes performance. Output on a Hermes runner matches Node.
- Deliberate English changes: business-zone times on staff screens and Connect, "p.m." on mobile, QST shown as 9.975%, "PST 7%" instead of "PST (BC) 7%", Connect opening hours as "9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.", non-CAD amounts as "US$12.50", and the tax setup "Collected so far" matching its quarter.
- Open: the zone label is web-only because the polyfill prints "GMT-7"; the polyfill's time zone data has British Columbia on permanent UTC−7 from 2026-11-01 while Node's ICU does not, so Vancouver times after that date differ by an hour between mobile and web or server until the data agrees. Metro was restarted with a cleared cache; the device check after a reload is pending.
- Checks: frontend typecheck and lint, app-core (243 tests under three time zones), api-client, site and playground suites pass; backend ruff, mypy, structure check and the full suite (1,394) pass.
